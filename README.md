# Music Genre Preferences Across Age Groups
## Overview
This project explores how music genre preferences vary across different age groups using survey data. It examines listening habits, favourite genres, and music consumption patterns to identify differences between age groups.

## Research Question
How do music genre preferences differ across age groups?

## Objectives
- Explore the distribution of participants across age groups.
- Identify the most commonly preferred music genres.
- Compare genre preferences between different age groups.
- Examine music listening habits and streaming service preferences.

## Dataset
The dataset contains 724 survey responses and includes age, favourite genre, streaming service, listening duration, and genre-listening frequency.
The public dataset version excludes the timestamp and unrelated survey variables.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Data cleaning and exploratory data analysis
- Data visualization
(Note: Python analysis may be added as the project develops.)

## Methodology
1. Prepared the survey dataset for analysis.
2. Examined participants' ages and listening habits.
3. Compared favourite genres across age groups.
4. Organized the findings into tables, charts, and a presentation.

## Key Findings
The analysis compared music preferences, streaming platforms, and listening habits across five age groups.

### 1. Non-Adults
- **Most-used streaming platform:** Spotify
- **Least-used streaming platform:** Pandora
- **Prefer listening while working:** Yes
- **Most common listening duration:** 2 hours
- **Most preferred genre:** Rock
- **Least preferred genre:** Latin
- **No recorded preference:** Gospel

### 2. Young Adults
- **Most-used streaming platform:** Spotify
- **Least-used streaming platform:** Pandora
- **Prefer listening while working:** Yes
- **Most common listening duration:** 2 hours
- **Most preferred genre:** Rock
- **Least preferred genres:** Latin and Gospel

### 3. Middle-Aged Adults
- **Most-used streaming platform:** Spotify
- **Least-used platforms:** Apple Music and Pandora
- **Prefer listening while working:** Yes
- **Most common listening durations:** 2 and 3 hours
- **Most preferred genre:** Rock
- **Least preferred genres:** EDM, Video Game Music, Country, and R&B
- **No recorded preference:** Hip-hop, Rap, K-pop, Jazz, Lo-fi, Latin, and Gospel

### 4. Seniors
- **Most-used platforms:** YouTube Music and other streaming services
- **Least-used platforms:** Pandora and Apple Music
- **Prefer listening while working:** Yes
- **Most common listening duration:** 2 hours
- **Most preferred genre:** Rock
- **Least preferred genres:** Classical, Hip-hop, and Jazz
- **No recorded preference:** EDM, Video Game Music, Country, Rap, K-pop, Lo-fi, Latin, Folk, and Metal

### 5. Elderly Participants
- **Streaming habits:** Most participants did not use a streaming service
- **Least-used platform:** Other streaming services
- **Prefer listening while working:** Yes
- **Most common listening duration:** 2 hours
- **Most preferred genre:** Rock
- **Least preferred genres:** Jazz, Country, Pop, and Rap
- **No recorded preference:** EDM, Video Game Music, K-pop, Lo-fi, Latin, Folk, and Metal

### Overall Observations
- Rock was the most preferred genre across all five age groups.
- Spotify was the most-used streaming platform among non-adults, young adults, and middle-aged adults.
- Two hours was the most common listening duration in every age group, although middle-aged adults also commonly listened for three hours.
- All five groups reported that they mostly preferred listening to music while working.
- Streaming platform usage differed among seniors and elderly participants compared with the younger groups.

(Note: “No recorded preference” means the analysis reported no preference for that genre; it does not necessarily mean participants actively disliked it.)
